CloudHorus only requires read permissions (Reader role) to visualize your Azure resources. For security best practices, assign the identity used here the Reader role at the appropriate scope. CloudHorus enforces a read-only network policy that blocks all write operations at the SDK level.

 What happened

            

          

          

            
 How to fix

            
    
              
  • Verify the identity (user, Service Principal, or Managed Identity) has the Reader role at the subscription or resource group scope.
    • 
              
  • If you switched accounts recently, sign out and authenticate again.
    • 
              
  • Check that the Tenant ID, Subscription ID, and Resource Group values are correct and aligned positionally.
    • 
              
  • For Service Principal auth: confirm AZURE_CLIENT_ID, AZURE_TENANT_ID, and AZURE_CLIENT_SECRET are valid and the SP exists in the target tenant.
